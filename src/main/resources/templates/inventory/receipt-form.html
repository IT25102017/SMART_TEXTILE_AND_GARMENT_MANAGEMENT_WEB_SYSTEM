Goods Receipt

Goods Receipt Number: GRN-001
A Goods Receipt requires at least one valid Purchase Order and one active Supplier from the Purchasing module.
Purchasing Information
Only available Purchase Orders are shown. Archived Purchase Orders are excluded.
The original Purchase Order cannot be changed after the Goods Receipt is created.
The Supplier is automatically matched when a Purchase Order is selected. The system also validates this relationship before saving.
The original Supplier cannot be changed after the Goods Receipt is created.
Receiving Information
Employee integration can later replace this field with an Employee dropdown.
Use the Restore button from the Goods Receipt list to reactivate this record.
